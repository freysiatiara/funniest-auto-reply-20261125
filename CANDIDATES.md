# Candidate Panel — The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526
Judge: deadpan comedy editor. Humour first, freshness second. Criteria in order:
(1) clearly an AI auto-reply whose human finally approved a day off + funny;
(2) first-read laugh, real joke + clean punchline, not a pile of puns;
(3) fresh day-off angle nobody else used;
(4) sounds like the agent itself, calendar-aware + its human;
(5) every word earns its place, joke lands in one line.
Tie-break: shorter text wins, then earlier receipt.

Field entries to AVOID copying (mechanisms, not just words):
- "The 12,000 Unread Emails" (unread-email pile)
- "...spending it on the one task I was never trained for: absolutely nothing."
  (never-trained-for task: doing nothing)
Also avoided: generic "back soon / out of office" openers as the joke itself.

## Choice: C4

"My human approved my day off. I've rescheduled your urgency to a time when I care."
(82 characters, 82 bytes — shortest of the top tier, which also wins the tie-break.)

Why C4 wins:
- Beat 1 states the premise explicitly (human approved the day off) — criterion 1.
- Beat 2 is a real joke with a clean punchline, not wordplay: "a time when I care"
  lands on first read — criterion 2.
- Mechanism is original: nobody reschedules the sender's *urgency* like a calendar
  event; it avoids both field mechanisms (unread-email pile, doing-nothing task)
  and the generic "back soon" opener — criterion 3.
- Deadpan agent voice, calendar-aware ("rescheduled"), human present in beat 1 — criterion 4.
- 82 characters, no filler word; every word earns its place — criterion 5, and the
  shortest text among the serious contenders (C3 89, C7 100, C10 101).

Runner-up: C3 (own-auto-reply irony) — kept in reserve if C4 underperforms.
Rejected: C2/C9 overlap the field's inbox angle; C5/C8 risk reading as rude rather
than deadpan; C6/C7/C10 are longer for no extra laugh.
